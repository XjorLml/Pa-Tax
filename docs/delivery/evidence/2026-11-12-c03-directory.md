## What changed

Prototype directory, to match the [SDD address contract](../../SDD.md#navigation-and-screen-state):
- The search box and service filter are one form. Typing changes nothing until Search is pressed or Enter is used. On wide screens each service button submits the form with its service. On phones the service menu applies when Search is pressed, and Search sits on its own full-width row under the menu.
- The sort menu became two links, Name (A–Z) and Highest rated. "Show more" became Previous and Next links with "Page N of M". Sort and page links carry the applied search and filter.
- The simulated address shows `q`, `service`, `sort` and `page`.
- Cards no longer show a per-user "Chatting" tag.
- Following a sort or page link replaces unsubmitted text in the search box with the applied search, as a browser's page load would.
