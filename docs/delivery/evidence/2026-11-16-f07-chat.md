14. Nothing was sent in the background.

**What the tests cover:**
- **Rule tests (on PostgreSQL):**
  - the text rule;
  - a first message creates the conversation, and the accountant has 1 unread;
  - one conversation per pair;
  - a repeated send stores one message;
  - opening marks the conversation read, and a reply is unread for the client;
  - nobody outside the conversation can read or write it, and reusing someone else's message ID is refused;
  - accountants can't start chats; unlisted and account-less profiles can't be messaged;
  - invalid text stores nothing;
  - 20 messages a minute, then "too fast";
  - an accountant sees the pictures of clients they chat with only;
  - a failed save reports failed, and the check says unknown.
- **Web tests:**
  - visitors are asked to sign in;
  - the visitor's new-chat page;
  - the accountant's list with "1 new" and the navigation count;
  - "Message" again goes to the same conversation;
  - the thread for both sides;
  - 404 "isn't available" for an outsider, with no names or text;
  - accountant, unknown and malformed new-chat addresses;
  - the client picture rule through `/pictures`.
