## The short version

- We collect what you give us, plus what Google shares when you sign in: your name, email address and a Google account identifier.
- Accountant profiles, portfolio highlights, reviews and replies are public, so anyone can see them. Chats and service work are private to the client and the accountant in them.

- Our service providers store data in Singapore, outside the Philippines, and we stay responsible for it there.
- You have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, including access, correction, objection and erasure. Contact our Data Protection Officer at lumaban.lemnuel.1@gmail.com.

## 2. What we collect

**When you sign in.** From Google, we receive your name, your email address and a Google account identifier. We never see your Google password. [OPTIONAL — include when Microsoft sign-in launches: the same details from Microsoft.]

**When you register.** Your role (client or accountant), and when you agreed to our [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md) and confirmed that you had read this policy, and to which version.

**Profile picture (optional).** If you add one, it can be a JPEG, PNG or WebP image of up to 5 MB. We save it as a 400 × 400 picture and remove its location and camera data. Without one, your initials are shown.

**Your details (clients).** The name we show for you, which starts as the name from Google and which you can change on your Account page. If you add them: a phone number, a city, and your business's name, type and industry. Please don't add your TIN or other government-issued numbers.

**Accountant profiles.** Your name, title, city, career summary, experience (role, company, and start and end month), services offered with short descriptions, and contact email, phone number and website. To be listed, you must agree to show your contact details publicly.

**Credentials.** Accountants describe their credentials in their own words. Under the DPA, licence details count as sensitive personal information, for example a PRC licence number or a licence's suspension or revocation. Please don't put your licence number or other government-issued numbers on your profile; give them to clients privately if they ask. The profile editor reminds you of this.

**Portfolio highlights.** Up to six, each with a title, the type of client, what was done and the result. A highlight must not name a client without that client's permission.

**Messages.** The text of each chat message (up to 2,000 characters), who sent it, when, and whether it has been read. Text you have typed but not sent stays in your browser tab only (section 8).

**Reviews and replies.** The star rating, any text, and when the review was written or changed; and the accountant's reply.

**Reports and blocks.** For a report: who sent it, what it is about (a profile, review or conversation), the reason, any details added, and when it was sent. For a block: when each person in a conversation blocked the other.

**Technical information.** Our application logs record only event codes, error categories, durations and correlation codes. They never record message text, email addresses, contact details or sign-in tokens. Our hosting provider may keep its own request logs, which can include IP addresses, for a limited time under its own policies.

**Saved accountants.** The accountants a client adds to their private list.

**Service work.** Service requests, checklists, task statuses, and shared notes with their author and time. Sending a request shares the client's name and the requested service with that accountant.

**Service documents.** Files the client or the accountant attaches to a service they share: the file, its name, size and type, who added it, when, and the task it belongs to. A document can be a PDF, JPEG or PNG file of up to 10 MB, and we check its content before keeping it. Documents may contain sensitive personal information, such as tax returns. They are kept in private storage, reached only through the Site. We don't scan them for viruses.

**Notification emails.** Whether you want emails about new messages, and a record of each email we queue or send, which is kept for 30 days. An email never includes the message text.

**Information about other people.** Messages, highlights and reviews can mention other people. Share only what you have the right to share ([Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md), section 9).

**What we don't collect.** We don't collect payment details. We don't use analytics or telemetry services, or advertising or tracking cookies.

## 4. What is public, and what is private

**Public.** Anyone can see the following, including people without an account:

- accountant profiles, including accountants' pictures and the contact details each accountant chooses to show;
- portfolio highlights;
- reviews (with the reviewer's first name and last initial), replies, average ratings and review counts.

Search engines may index public pages and show them in their results. While the Site is limited to invited testers, it asks search engines not to index it. Anyone can copy public content, and we can't recall copies.

**Shared with specific people.**

- Your name: with the people you chat with.
- A client's picture: with that client and the accountants they have a conversation with.
- A client's details (phone, city and business): with that client and the accountants they have a service with.
- Messages: with the conversation's client and accountant only.
- Service work and service documents: with that service's client and accountant only.
- Saved accountants: with that client only.

**Seen only by us.** The email address from your Google account (unless an accountant also publishes it as their contact email), your Google identifier, your agreement record, reports and who sent them, and blocks. The other person does notice a block's effect, because they can no longer send messages.

Client accounts are never listed publicly.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Account, profile and picture:** while your account exists.
- **Unused accounts:** if you don't use your account for 2 years, we email you a warning and then delete it, as below.
- **When an account is deleted:** we remove its name, email, profile picture, a client's details and sign-in links, and an accountant's profile. What remains is a nameless record, shown as "Deleted user", that the other person's chats point to.
- **Messages after an account is deleted:** they stay with the other person in the conversation, shown as from "Deleted user". When both people in a conversation have deleted their accounts, the conversation is deleted.
- **Reviews and replies after an account is deleted:** a client's reviews are removed, and ratings are recalculated. An accountant's profile is removed with its reviews and replies.
- **Service work and documents after an account is deleted:** documents someone uploaded are deleted with their account. The rest of the service work, such as requests, checklists and notes, stays with the other person, shown as from "Deleted user". A client's saved accountants are deleted.
- **Reports:** kept while we handle the report and any follow-up, then for 12 months.
- **Logs and backups:** our hosting and database providers keep them for a limited time under their own policies, and older copies are replaced in turn.
- **Cookies:** see section 8.

You can delete your account from your Account page, or ask us to by emailing lumaban.lemnuel.1@gmail.com. We may keep information for longer where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

## 8. Cookies and browser storage

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts | Personal information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in cookie | Keeps you signed in. Sent only over HTTPS, and can't be read by scripts | 14 days, renewed each time you use the Site; removed when you sign out | Identifies your account |
| First sign-in cookie | Holds your Google sign-in while you finish registering | A few minutes | Identifies your sign-in |

| Theme cookie (`taxbuddy-theme`) | Remembers light or dark mode | One year; not set if you choose "System" | None |

**Browser storage.** Text you type in a chat stays in that browser tab's session storage until you send it, so it survives a reload. It isn't sent to us until you press Send, and the browser clears it when you close the tab.

All of these are needed for the Site to work, or to remember a setting you chose. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and the Site serves its own fonts. During sign-in, Google's [OPTIONAL — or Microsoft's] pages may set their own cookies under their own policies.

## 9. How we protect it

- The Site is served over HTTPS, and the sign-in cookie can't be read by scripts.
- We don't hold passwords. You sign in with Google.
- Our server checks access to every private item. Only a conversation's two participants can read it, whatever link is used.
- Profile pictures are kept in private storage, reached only through the Site, and their location and camera data is removed.
- Service documents are kept in private storage, reached only through the Site. Only the service's client and accountant can download them.
- Our logs leave out message text, email addresses, contact details and tokens.
- We limit how often messages and reports can be sent.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take these steps to protect your information.

## 11. Your rights

Under the DPA, you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how we process your personal information, as this policy does;
- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **object** to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete information;
- **erasure or blocking** of information that is incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or no longer needed;
- **data portability**, meaning a copy of your information in a commonly used electronic format;
- **damages**, if you suffer harm from inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false or unlawfully obtained information, or from its unauthorised use;
- **complain** to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Your lawful heirs and assigns may exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.

**Doing it yourself.** You can edit your profile or your details, change or remove your picture, edit or delete your reviews, block someone, and withdraw your agreement to show contact details. Withdrawing that agreement takes your profile out of the directory. You can also delete your account from your Account page. You can also turn off notification emails, in Account or from the link in any email.

**Asking us.** Email our Data Protection Officer at lumaban.lemnuel.1@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first, for example by asking you to write from your account's email address. We'll respond within 15 working days, free of charge.

**Limits.** We may refuse or limit a request where the law allows, for example to protect other people's rights, such as the other person's side of a conversation, or to establish or defend legal claims. If we do, we'll tell you why.

## 12. Children

The Site is not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly let anyone under that age register. If you believe someone under that age has an account, tell us at lumaban.lemnuel.1@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

## 13. Accountants and their clients' information

When a client shares personal information with an accountant for professional work, the accountant decides how it is used. The accountant is responsible for it under the DPA and their professional duties. We store messages and service work so the Site can deliver them. We use their content for nothing else, apart from handling reports (section 3) and complying with the law.

Please don't send your Taxpayer Identification Number, tax returns, government ID numbers or financial records in chat; a reminder above the message box says so. To share documents for a service your accountant has accepted, attach them to that service instead, where only the two of you can download them.
