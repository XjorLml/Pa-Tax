## 10. Reviews and replies

**Who can review.** A client can review an accountant once that accountant has sent at least one message in their conversation. **How reviews appear.** Reviews are public and show the reviewer's first name and last initial. Each profile shows the accountant's average rating and number of reviews, and the directory can be ordered by rating.

**Replies.** The accountant can post one public reply to each review, of up to 600 characters. They can edit the reply but not delete it. An accountant can't edit, hide or delete a review.

**Blocking and reviews.** If an accountant has blocked a client, that client can't write or edit a review of that accountant, but can still delete their own review.

**Standards.** A review must describe your own genuine experience. State facts accurately, and make clear what is your opinion. Replies must stay professional and must not disclose a client's confidential information. We don't edit reviews or replies, but we may remove one that breaks these Terms (section 12).
