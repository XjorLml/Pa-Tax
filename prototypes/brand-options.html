Name and logo options

Fourteen names for the accountant directory (working name "AccApp"), each with a logo sketch: six in Taglish, then eight in Filipino. They lean on what paying taxes in the Philippines feels like: the receipts, the April deadline, the hassle, and wanting someone you can trust to sort it out.

These are sketches to choose a direction, not finished artwork. A quick web search on 11 November 2026 found no tax or accounting product using these names. It was not a trademark, business-name or domain check.

Tax Kalma Taglish pick

tax + kalma (relax)

"Kalma lang, may accountant ka na."

Relax, you've got an accountant now.

Meaning
"Kalma lang" is what a friend says when you panic. Here it answers the tax panic.
Logo
A sun over calm water.
Why it fits
Names the struggle and the relief in two words, and stays respectable enough for an accountant's profile.
Watch out
Sells the feeling, not the ratings. The tagline has to carry "accountant".

May Resibo Overall pick

"mai reh-SEE-boh" · has receipts

"Mga accountant na may resibo."

Accountants with the receipts to prove it.

Meaning
A resibo is the receipt the BIR keeps asking for. In everyday slang, "may resibo" means "I have proof".
Logo
A receipt with a torn edge and a gold star: proof plus rating.
Why it fits
The site's whole point is ratings and portfolios, which are an accountant's receipts.
Watch out
Could be mistaken for a receipt-scanning app. The tagline has to say "accountants".

Tagatuos Formal alternative

"Hanapin ang tagatuos na bagay sa'yo."

Find the accountant who suits you.

Meaning
The Tagalog word for accountant, from tuos: to reckon and settle accounts.

Abril Kinse

April fifteen

"Handa ka na bago mag-Abril Kinse."

Ready before April 15.

Before you settle on one

  • Search the name in the IPOPHL trademark database, and in the DTI or SEC business-name search.
  • Check the domain (.ph, .com.ph, .com) and the social media handles.
  • Say it aloud to three accountants and three clients. Accountants have to want their name next to it.
  • The colours here are placeholders. The final mark should take its colour from the look you choose for the site.
